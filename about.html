About me

Simple ideas. Strong design.

I design brands, social media and digital products that are simple, clear and powerful.

I started with a graphic design certificate in 2019. At school, I started Simply Creations with three students I had trained.

My work starts with research and sketches and ends as 3D models, print layouts and screens. I care about clarity: if a user or reader has to stop and think about how something works, the design isn't finished.

Simply Creations was minimal and simple from the start: brand designs, social media posts and freelance work from 2021 to 2024. After a break from design, starting my diploma at PSB Academy brought me back. Now I work under my own name, with Simply Creations as my studio.

Today I take on freelance projects while studying for my Diploma in Graphic Design & Media at PSB Academy.

My studio

Simply Creations

At HCCPAS, the Henegama Central College Photographic Arts Society, I was the main designer. During my A/Levels I realised I was the only one doing design, so I brought in new students and trained them in Photoshop, Illustrator and design principles. From that group I started Simply Creations: four of us, with one aim, simple, powerful and neat design.

The logo shows four people around a table, each bringing a different idea. It is still my studio today. Simple by design. See how the logo was made
