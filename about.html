About me

Designing games that feel clear and fun.

I design the look and feel of games: characters, worlds, interfaces and the brands around them.

I started with a graphic design certificate in 2019 and have worked as a designer ever since.

My work starts with research and sketches and ends as 3D models, print layouts and screens. I care about clarity: if a player or reader has to stop and think about how something works, the design isn't finished.

Today I take on freelance projects while studying for my Diploma in Design Media at PSB Academy.

エイドリアン

EIDORIAN comes from my name, Adrian, written in Japanese katakana as エイドリアン (Ei-do-ri-an). I chose a kanji for each sound. Read together, 英道理庵 means "the hero's way, a studio of craft".

英
EiExcellence, hero
道
DōThe way, as in bushidō
理
RiLogic, reason
庵
AnA retreat, an artist's studio
