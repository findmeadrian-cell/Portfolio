How I work

From research to result

Every project follows the same four steps, whether it ends as a 3D model, a magazine or an app. Here is what happens in each one, and how it played out in real projects.

  1. Research

    Mood boards, references and, for UI/UX work, talking to the people who will use it.

  2. Sketch

    Fast thumbnails and wireframes first, then a few strong ideas developed further.

  3. Build

    Ideas become 3D models, page layouts or clickable prototypes.

  4. Test and finish

    Check it with real users or readers, fix what doesn't work, then polish materials, type and detail.

Step by step

Inside each step

01

Research

Understand the problem before designing anything.

I start by asking who it is for and what has to change for them. Then I collect references, look at competitors and build a mood board. For UI/UX work I write a user persona and map the journey they take.

What you get

  • A clear brief in a few lines
  • Mood board and references
  • Persona and user journey (UI/UX)

Tools

  • Pinterest
  • Figma
  • Notes and interviews

02

Sketch

Explore many ideas quickly, on paper first.

I fill pages with fast thumbnails: logos, layouts, posts or screens. Bad ideas are cheap on paper, so I push past the first ones. The strongest two or three get developed into cleaner sketches or wireframes.

What you get

  • Thumbnail sketches
  • Two or three chosen directions
  • Wireframes and sitemap (UI/UX)

Tools

  • Pencil and sketchbook
  • Figma wireframes

03

Build

Turn the chosen idea into the real thing.

The chosen direction becomes vector artwork, a 3D model, a page layout or a clickable prototype. This is where type, colour and materials are decided and a small design system is set up so everything stays consistent.

What you get

  • Final artwork or model
  • Colour, type and style guide
  • Clickable prototype or print-ready layout

Tools

  • Illustrator
  • Photoshop
  • InDesign
  • Rhino
  • Blender
  • Figma

04

Test and finish

Check it works, then polish every detail.

I test the design with real people or in real places: on a phone, printed at size, or as a mockup on a sign. What doesn't work gets fixed. Then I polish the details and prepare files and guidelines so the work can be used correctly.

What you get

  • Feedback and changes
  • Mockups in context
  • Final files and brand guidelines

Tools

  • Mockups
  • After Effects
  • Prototype testing

What guides me

Three rules I design by

01

Clarity first

If someone has to stop and think about how it works, the design isn't finished.

02

Paper before pixels

Sketching many ideas fast leads to better ideas than polishing the first one.

03

Every detail has a reason

Each colour, typeface and shape should be explainable, not just decoration.

Working with me

How a freelance project runs

  1. Chat

    We talk about your goal, audience, budget and deadline.

  2. Proposal

    I send the scope, timeline and price for you to approve.

  3. Concepts

    You see two or three directions and pick one.

  4. Refine

    We go through agreed rounds of feedback.

  5. Handover

    You get final files in every format you need, plus usage notes.
