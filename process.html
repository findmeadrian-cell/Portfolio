How I work

From research to result

Every project follows the same four steps, whether it ends as a 3D model, a magazine or an app.

  1. Research

    Mood boards, references and, for UI/UX work, talking to the people who will use it.

  2. Sketch

    Fast thumbnails and wireframes first, then a few strong ideas developed further.

  3. Build

    Ideas become 3D models, page layouts or clickable prototypes.

  4. Test and finish

    Check it with real users or readers, fix what doesn't work, then polish materials, type and detail.
