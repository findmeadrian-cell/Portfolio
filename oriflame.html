Case file · Social media

Oriflame Sri Lanka

Social media posts for Oriflame cosmetics in Sri Lanka, used to promote beauty workshops, offers and business opportunities.

Project
Social media and promotional posts
Client
Oriflame Sri Lanka (cosmetics)
Studio
Simply Creations
Year
2023

01The brief

Oriflame Sri Lanka needed posts to fill seats at makeup and skin care workshops across the country, and to promote offers and part-time business opportunities. The posts were used on social media and in their promotions.

02The approach

Pink and plum to match the beauty world, big clear headlines, and the date, venue and price easy to find. Many posts are in Sinhala, or Sinhala and English, so they speak directly to a Sri Lankan audience. Each event got a small series of posts in the same style.

Designed at Simply Creations (2021–2024), the minimalist design studio I founded with three students I trained at HCCPAS. Simple by design.
