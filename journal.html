01 Week 01

Communication & Costing

Week 01 · 21 September 2026

Communication Exercise

01 Research

The exercise asked us to write to a client. I based mine on a kind of job I really get as a freelancer: a client who wanted me to design a cartoon book.

02 Process

In my reply I explained what I would design and quoted one price for the whole book, so the client could see the full cost before we started.

03 Reflection

Talking to the client is part of the design job, not something separate from it. Being clear about what is included and what it costs at the start avoids confusion later.

04 Outcome

A client reply for a cartoon book project, with the scope and a full-book price.

Week 01 · 21 September 2026

Costing / Pricing Exercise

01 Research

I used the same cartoon book job and thought about the time and work a full book takes as a single project.

02 Process

I priced the full book as one project instead of charging page by page. My final price was about $500.

03 Reflection

Pricing is about more than getting paid. If I charge too little, it lowers what clients expect to pay every designer, so I want my prices to reflect the real work.

04 Outcome

Final quote: about $500 for the complete cartoon book.

02 Week 02

Work Ethics

Week 02 · 28 September 2026

Ethics Reflection

01 Research

This week we looked at the ethics of working as a designer and how our choices affect clients and other designers.

02 Process

I thought about which rule matters most to me from my freelance work since 2020.

03 Reflection

My rule is that I don't design for free or for very low prices, like under $20. If I accept that once, the client will expect the next designer to work for even less. Cheap work hurts the whole design industry, not just me.

04 Outcome

My working principle: charge fairly for every job, so design work keeps its value.

Week 02 · 28 September 2026

Ethical Scenario Responses

01 Research

The trickiest scenario for me was a customer asking for a design for free or for under $20.

02 Process

It is tempting to say yes to keep the customer happy or win the job. I thought about what happens after that job is done.

03 Reflection

If I say yes, the customer learns that design is cheap and expects the same from other designers. Saying no politely and explaining my price protects me and other designers.

04 Outcome

My response: turn down free or very low-paid work, and explain why my price is what it is.

03 Week 03

Future Gazing & Promotion

Week 03 · 5 October 2026

10-Year Career Plan

01 Research

I looked at the kinds of design work I enjoy and that clients ask me for, beyond graphic design alone.

02 Process

My plan is to build skills in several areas: 3D, animation, 3D printing, T-shirt printing and videography.

03 Reflection

Knowing different sides of design means I can give customers more than one kind of solution, and understand how a design works across print, screen and physical products.

04 Outcome

In ten years I want to have mastered these paths and be a designer who can take an idea from sketch to 3D, motion, print and video.

Week 03 · 5 October 2026

Professional Introduction Exercise

01 Research

I thought about what makes me different from other designers: I don't stay in one area.

02 Process

I kept my introduction to one sentence that says who I am and what I offer.

03 Reflection

A short introduction is easier to remember than a long list of skills. It also has to match my brand, EIDORIAN.

04 Outcome

"I'm Adrian Reyhart, a designer working across graphics, 3D, animation and print, so I can give clients more than one side of design."

04 Week 04

Constraints & Workflow

Week 04 · 12 October 2026

Constraints Exercise

01 Research

My main constraint is one I set for myself: a limited colour palette.

02 Process

I use a maximum of two to four colours in a design, and I build my designs from my own sketch art instead of stock images.

03 Reflection

Limiting colours makes me focus on shape, layout and type, and keeps the design minimal and clear. Using my own sketches makes every design original.

04 Outcome

My rule: two to four colours per design, built from my own sketches.

Week 04 · 12 October 2026

Workflow Exercise

01 Research

I looked at how I already work on my freelance projects.

02 Process

I follow minimal step-by-step guides for each design: start with my own sketches, choose a limited colour palette of two to four colours, then build the final design.

03 Reflection

Having the same steps every time saves time and keeps my work consistent. The Process page of this website shows the full version: research, sketch, build, test and finish.

04 Outcome

My workflow: sketch, limit colours, build, refine.

05 Week 05

Personal Branding

Week 05 · 19 October 2026

Brand Foundation

01 Research

Japanese culture is one of the main inspirations for my designs.

02 Process

My English name didn't feel like it fit the design world. When I converted it into Japanese, エイドリアン (Eidorian), it matched both the design world and the culture that inspires me.

03 Reflection

A brand name should feel like me and connect to where my ideas come from. EIDORIAN does both.

04 Outcome

Brand name: EIDORIAN. Written in kanji as 英道理庵: hero, way, reason, studio.

Week 05 · 19 October 2026

Personal Brand Statement

01 Research

I built my statement from my career plan and my working rules.

02 Process

I combined three things: the many areas of design I work in, my minimal style, and my Japanese inspiration.

03 Reflection

The statement needs to tell a client quickly what I do and how I work.

04 Outcome

"EIDORIAN is the design identity of Adrian Reyhart: minimal, sketch-based design across graphics, 3D, animation and print, inspired by Japanese culture."

Week 05 · 19 October 2026

Visual Identity

01 Research

I wanted my identity to follow my own rules: minimal, and only a few colours.

02 Process

I chose black, white and red, which keeps me within my two-to-four colour rule. For type I chose Archivo Expanded for headings and Helvetica for body text, because they are clean and modern.

03 Reflection

This website is part of my visual identity. I tried more decorative styles first, but kept the site minimal so my work stands out.

04 Outcome

Colours: black, white and red. Type: Archivo Expanded and Helvetica. Name: ADRIAN REYHART in capitals, with EIDORIAN as my identity.

Week 05 · 19 October 2026

Brand Rationale

01 Research

This post connects the work from Weeks 01 to 05.

02 Process

Each part of my brand comes from my own working rules: fair pricing (Weeks 01 and 02), working across many areas of design (Week 03), a limited colour palette and my own sketches (Week 04), and Japanese inspiration (Week 05).

03 Reflection

Before this module, my design work and my brand were separate. Now my name, colours, type and website all say the same thing about how I work.

04 Outcome

Next, for CW02, I will add my real projects, my 3D and animation work, and grow this website into my full professional portfolio.
