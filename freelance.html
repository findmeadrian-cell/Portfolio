Freelance Designs

Song covers, logos, invitations and greeting posts made for clients as a freelancer.

Project
Freelance design work
Clients
Musicians, small businesses, clubs and families in Sri Lanka
Work
Song covers, logos, invitations, social posts
Years
2021–2024

01The work

Alongside bigger brand projects, I took on everyday design jobs: covers for new songs, logos for small businesses, a wedding invitation and greeting posts for festivals.

02The approach

Each job had its own style, but the aim was always the same: one clear idea, type that fits the mood, and every detail easy to read. Many pieces use Sinhala script, because they were made for Sri Lankan audiences.
